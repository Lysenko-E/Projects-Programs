Dry (weather or something that has dried completely) ⭐⭐⭐ ⏎  ⏎ This is the most common meaning. ⏎  ⏎ It describes something that is: ⏎  ⏎ pleasantly dry ⏎ free of humidity ⏎ crisp and dry ⏎ Examples ⏎ 今日はからっとした天気だ。 ⏎ → It's a nice, dry day today. ⏎ 洗濯物がからっと乾いた。 ⏎ → The laundry dried completely. ⏎ 空気がからっとしている。 ⏎ → The air is nice and dry. ⏎  ⏎ This is a very common word in Japan, especially during the humid summer when people appreciate からっとした天気. ⏎  ⏎ 2. Cheerfully / without dwelling on things ⏎  ⏎ When describing a person's personality or attitude, からっと means: ⏎  ⏎ cheerfully ⏎ without holding a grudge ⏎ openly ⏎ with no lingering negativity ⏎  ⏎ Examples ⏎ 彼はからっとした性格だ。 ⏎ → He has an easygoing, cheerful personality. ⏎ 失敗してもからっと笑った。 ⏎ → Even after failing, she just laughed it off. ⏎ からっとしていて付き合いやすい人だ。 ⏎ → He's open and easy to get along with. ⏎  ⏎ Here the image is that the person's emotions are not "damp" or heavy—they recover quickly and don't brood over things. |

ひょっと | possibly | ひょっと | ひょっとすると雨かも。 | Might rain. | ひょっとしたら | もしかして ⏎ ひょっと is an adverb that almost always appears in one of these patterns: ⏎  ⏎ ひょっとすると ⏎ ひょっとしたら ⏎ ひょっとして ⏎  ⏎ It means: ⏎  ⏎ perhaps ⏎ maybe ⏎ possibly ⏎ by any chance ⏎  ⏎ It expresses that something unexpected or uncertain might be true. ⏎  ⏎ 1. ひょっとすると ⭐⭐⭐ (most common) ⏎  ⏎ Perhaps... ⏎ It's possible that... ⏎  ⏎ Examples: ⏎  ⏎ ひょっとすると、彼はもう帰ったかもしれない。 ⏎ → Perhaps he has already gone home. ⏎ ひょっとすると雨が降るかも。 ⏎ → Maybe it'll rain. ⏎ 2. ひょっとしたら ⏎  ⏎ This has the same meaning and is also very common. ⏎  ⏎ ひょっとしたら合格できるかもしれない。 ⏎ → Maybe I can pass. ⏎ ひょっとしたら彼も来るかも。 ⏎ → Perhaps he'll come too. ⏎ 3. ひょっとして ⏎  ⏎ Often used when asking a question or making a guess. ⏎  ⏎ ひょっとして日本人ですか？ ⏎ → Are you Japanese, by any chance? ⏎ ひょっとして忘れた？ ⏎ → Did you forget, by any chance? ⏎  ⏎ This is extremely common in conversation. ⏎  ⏎ Difference from もしかして ⏎  ⏎ ひょっとして and もしかして are very similar. ⏎  ⏎ もしかして、鍵を忘れた？ ⏎ → Did you forget your keys, by any chance? ⏎ ひょっとして、鍵を忘れた？ ⏎ → Did you forget your keys, by any chance? ⏎  ⏎ もしかして is probably a bit more common in everyday speech, while ひょっとして can sound a little softer or slightly more literary, but in most situations they're interchangeable. ⏎  ⏎ Easy way to remember ⏎  ⏎ Think of ひょっと as introducing an unexpected possibility. ⏎  ⏎ ひょっとすると… ⏎ → Perhaps... ⏎ ひょっとしたら… ⏎ → Maybe... ⏎ ひょっとして…？ ⏎ → By any chance...? |
