樹立 | じゅりつ | establishment | 新記録を樹立した。 | A new record was set. | 記録樹立 | 樹立（じゅりつ） means to establish, to set up, or to create firmly something important, formal, or enduring. ⏎  ⏎ It's a formal word often used in news, business, politics, sports, and diplomacy. ⏎  ⏎ Common collocations ⏎ 記録を樹立する ⏎ → to set a record ⏎ 新記録を樹立する ⏎ → to establish a new record ⏎ 外交関係を樹立する ⏎ → to establish diplomatic relations ⏎ 体制を樹立する ⏎ → to establish a system/regime ⏎ 地位を樹立する ⏎ → to establish a position/status ⏎ Examples ⏎ 彼は世界新記録を樹立した。 ⏎ → He set a new world record. ⏎ 両国は外交関係を樹立した。 ⏎ → The two countries established diplomatic relations. ⏎ 業界で確固たる地位を樹立した。 ⏎ → They established a solid position in the industry. ⏎ Comparison with 設立（せつりつ） ⏎ 設立 = establish an organization/company ⏎ 会社を設立する ⏎ 学校を設立する ⏎ 樹立 = establish something more abstract, prestigious, or institutional ⏎ 記録を樹立する ⏎ 外交関係を樹立する ⏎ 体制を樹立する ⏎  ⏎ A useful image is that 樹立 literally contains the character for "tree" (樹), giving the sense of planting something firmly and making it stand. It's much more formal than everyday 「作る」 or 「設ける」. | 
成立 | せいりつ | establishment; conclusion | 契約が成立した。 | The contract was finalized. | 成立条件 | 成立（せいりつ） means to be established, to come into effect, to be concluded, or to be successfully formed, depending on the context. ⏎  ⏎ The core idea is: ⏎  ⏎ "something comes together successfully and becomes valid or complete." ⏎  ⏎ Common uses ⏎ 1. Laws, bills, agreements ⏎ 法案が成立する ⏎ → a bill passes / becomes law ⏎ 契約が成立する ⏎ → a contract is concluded ⏎ 交渉が成立する ⏎ → negotiations are successfully concluded ⏎  ⏎ Example: ⏎  ⏎ 国会で法案が成立した。 ⏎ → The bill passed in the Diet. ⏎ 2. Conditions being met ⏎ 条件が成立する ⏎ → conditions are satisfied ⏎ この理論は成立しない。 ⏎ → This theory doesn't hold up / isn't valid. ⏎  ⏎ Example: ⏎  ⏎ この場合、その仮説は成立しない。 ⏎ → In this case, that hypothesis is not valid. ⏎ 3. Relationships, situations ⏎ 結婚が成立する ⏎ → a marriage is legally established ⏎ ビジネスが成立する ⏎ → a business deal works out / becomes viable ⏎  ⏎ Example: ⏎  ⏎ 双方が合意すれば契約が成立します。 ⏎ → The contract is concluded once both parties agree. ⏎ Difference from similar words ⏎ 設立（せつりつ） ⏎ establish an organization ⏎ 会社を設立する ⏎ 樹立（じゅりつ） ⏎ establish something prestigious/formal ⏎ 記録を樹立する ⏎ 成立（せいりつ） ⏎ become valid, complete, or successfully formed ⏎ 契約が成立する ⏎ 法案が成立する ⏎ Example sentences ⏎ 契約は昨日成立しました。 ⏎ → The contract was concluded yesterday. ⏎ この理論は十分に成立している。 ⏎ → This theory is well-founded. ⏎ 条件が成立すれば実施します。 ⏎ → We will proceed if the conditions are met. ⏎  ⏎ A useful way to remember it: ⏎  ⏎ 成立 = "to come into force / become valid / successfully come together." |
