You'll see it constantly in business, academia, news, and novels. ⏎  ⏎ Its core meaning is: ⏎  ⏎ premise ⏎ assumption ⏎ precondition ⏎ something taken as a given ⏎  ⏎ Core image ⏎  ⏎ Think of 前提 as: ⏎  ⏎ "the foundation that comes before the discussion." ⏎  ⏎ If that foundation changes, the conclusion may also change. ⏎  ⏎ 1. Assumption ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (most common) ⏎ その前提が違う。 ⏎  ⏎ That assumption is wrong. ⏎  ⏎ 前提として ⏎  ⏎ As a premise... ⏎ On the assumption that... ⏎  ⏎ Example: ⏎  ⏎ 安全を前提として計画を立てる。 ⏎  ⏎ We make the plan on the assumption that safety comes first. ⏎  ⏎ 2. Precondition ⏎ 参加が前提となる。 ⏎  ⏎ Participation is a prerequisite. ⏎  ⏎ 合意が前提だ。 ⏎  ⏎ Agreement is a precondition. ⏎  ⏎ 3. Premise (logic) ⏎  ⏎ Used in philosophy and logical arguments. ⏎  ⏎ Example: ⏎  ⏎ その結論は前提が間違っている。 ⏎  ⏎ That conclusion is based on a faulty premise. ⏎  ⏎ Common expressions ⏎ 前提とする ⏎  ⏎ to assume ⏎ to take as a premise ⏎  ⏎ Example: ⏎  ⏎ 経験があることを前提としている。 ⏎  ⏎ It assumes that you have experience. ⏎  ⏎ 前提になる ⏎  ⏎ to become a prerequisite |
