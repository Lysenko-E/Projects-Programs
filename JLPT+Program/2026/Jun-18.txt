The focus is on the result—the culture gradually developed. ⏎  ⏎ Difference from 育てられた ⏎  ⏎ Both can translate as "raised," but the nuance differs. ⏎  ⏎ 育てられた（そだてられた） ⏎  ⏎ Focuses on being raised or brought up by someone. ⏎  ⏎ 親に育てられた。 ⏎ → I was raised by my parents. ⏎ 育まれた ⏎  ⏎ Focuses on gradual nurturing and development. ⏎  ⏎ 信頼が育まれた。 ⏎ → Trust was cultivated. ⏎ 文化が育まれた。 ⏎ → Culture was nurtured over time. ⏎  ⏎ You usually don't say: ⏎  ⏎ ❌ 親に育まれた ⏎  ⏎ Instead: ⏎  ⏎ ✅ 親に育てられた ⏎  ⏎ because 育む is more about fostering growth, while 育てる is the ordinary verb for raising a child. ⏎  ⏎ Common collocations ⏎  ⏎ You'll often see: ⏎  ⏎ 愛情に育まれる ⏎ → be nurtured by love ⏎ 自然に育まれる ⏎ → be nurtured by nature ⏎ 文化が育まれる ⏎ → culture develops ⏎ 信頼が育まれる ⏎ → trust is built ⏎ 絆が育まれる ⏎ → bonds are strengthened ⏎ 豊かな感性が育まれる ⏎ → rich sensitivity is cultivated |
